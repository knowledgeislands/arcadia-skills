## Dependency and ownership

`ki-repo-kb-principal` is a small overlay on `ki-repo-kb` and `ki-decision-records`. Its five-file checklist gives useful orientation for a governance home, root memory anchor, local conventions, Known Lands, and an Enactment entry point. Arcadia and Techne both compose the six selected audits cleanly.

Principal status itself is neither represented nor verified. Both estates declare the overlay despite its singular canonical-island wording, and no selected role, authority, or cross-island relationship contract exists. Any future identity mechanism belongs with `ki-repo` and the Agora/trades owners. The overlay also inherits the unresolved KB and Decision Record metadata collision.

## Mechanical trace and limits

The native audit checks five regular files and one `/Enactment Process|enactment gate/i` regex. Five empty files plus a keyword can therefore pass. It does not validate content, links, local authority, selected adapter, approved record, lifecycle state, or relationship integrity; conform has no writes.

Two tests cover missing files and the keyword fixture. There is no exact eval, outcome evidence, source registry, refresh cadence, or negative content/approval fixture. Arcadia's strong local process is evidence about that estate, not something the portable checker proves.

## Candidate improvements

1. Retain structural orientation but classify principal/canonical authority as unproven until the identity owners define one authoritative representation.
2. After that decision, validate only the permitted selected-adapter and Enactment-gate evidence; never infer approval from a phrase.
3. Add meaningful non-empty, link, relationship, source, and positive/negative outcome evidence within the eventual ownership boundary.
4. Reconcile inherited KB/Decision Record metadata before relying on aggregate principal completion.

## Applied changes

The checker now reports structural entry points only. Empty files and keyword-only prose cannot imply a governing gate, canonical authority, or principal role. Identity and cross-island authority representation remain unresolved owner decisions.

## Carry-forward criteria

A role overlay may prove structural orientation only. It must not claim canonical authority, cross-repository identity, or transition eligibility without the selected owner's representation and executable evidence.
